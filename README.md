# Flow-Audit Agent (for Claude)

A free lifecycle and email flow auditor. It finds the moves in your email program that feel like a win but lose money, then hands back a fix list ranked by the revenue and retention each one protects.

Built by Utsav, who runs creative and lifecycle marketing at a B2B agency.

## How to use it (about 2 minutes)
1. Open a new chat at https://claude.ai
2. Open `flow-audit-claude-prompt.md`, copy the whole file.
3. Paste it as your first message in Claude.
4. Answer the few questions it asks (your ESP, list size, which flows are live, any numbers). Rough answers are fine.
5. It audits your flows and gives you a prioritized fix list.

It never sees your account and stores nothing. It only reads what you tell it.

## Prefer a Claude Project?
Paste `claude-project-instructions.md` into a Project's custom instructions and add `knowledge.md` as project knowledge.

## What it checks
Deliverability, list hygiene and suppression, segmentation, and each live flow (welcome, cart, browse, post-purchase, win-back, sunset). Benchmarks are 2026 industry reference points, labeled with sources, to compare against your own numbers.
